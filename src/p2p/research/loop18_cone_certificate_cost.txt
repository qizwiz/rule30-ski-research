KEY INSIGHT DISCOVERED:
  The caStepList/caEvolve approach SHRINKS the list by 2 per step.
  The period certificate checks:
    caEvolve P_m (spike_m_list(2*P_m + 2*m + 1)) == spike_m_list(2*m + 1)
  Input size: 2*P_m + 2*m + 1
  Actual work: sum_{k=0}^{P_m-1} (2*P_m + 2*m + 1 - 2*k) = P_m*(P_m + 2*m + 1)
  This is O(P_m²), not O(P_m × (2m+1)).

  In Lean native_decide (compiled, ~0.3-3ns/op): ~3-30s estimated.
