PROOF (frontier-chain argument):
  Frontier invariant (proved by induction on j):
    At step j, all cells at positions < 2n'+2-j are 0.
    Proof: any cell p < 2n'+2-j has l=c=r=0 at step j-1 (all left of frontier),
           so rule30(0,0,0) = 0 XOR (0 OR 0) = 0.

  Chain:
    At step n'+1, center n'+1:
      l = cell[n']@n' = 0 (by frontier invariant, n' < 2n'+2-n' = n'+2)
      c = cell[n'+1]@n' = 0 (by frontier invariant, n'+1 < n'+2)
      r = cell[n'+2]@n' (frontier cell at step n')
    → center@(n'+1) = rule30(0,0,r) = r = cell[n'+2]@n'

    Applying recursively: cell[n'+2]@n' = cell[n'+3]@(n'-1) = ... = cell[2n'+2]@0 = 1

    At each step, the frontier cell's only nontrivial neighbor is its right neighbor
    (which is the previous frontier), giving the direct copy chain.

ALGEBRAIC STRUCTURE (new insight):
  G(n',m) = F(n',m) XOR H(n') XOR I(n',m)  [definition of I]
  Since H=1:  G = NOT(F XOR I) = F XOR 1 XOR I

  SubcaseB ↔ F=0 AND G=1
           ↔ F=0 AND (F XOR 1 XOR I = 1)
           ↔ F=0 AND (0 XOR 1 XOR I = 1)
           ↔ F=0 AND I=0

  Nonlinear interaction:
    I(n',m) = F(n',m) XOR G(n',m) XOR 1
    Active m: I=0 sometimes (when F=0) → SubcaseB possible
    Inactive m: I=1 always → G=F → SubcaseB impossible
